Troubleshooting

Common issue fixes & setup tips

HUB ISSUES

Dashboard says "Hub Disconnected"

Start With Power
• Make sure your hub is plugged in and switched on.
• Check that the power outlet is live.
• Unplug the hub, wait 10 seconds, and plug it back in.
Check The Cables
• Follow each sensor cable from the aquarium to the hub and press any loose plugs firmly back in.
• Look for cracked or damaged cable. A damaged cable will need replacing.
• Make sure the hub itself is dry and above the waterline.
Give It A Moment
• After replugging, the hub may take 1 to 2 minutes to reappear. Close the app and reopen it to refresh the dashboard.
READING ISSUES

Readings are Wrong or Stagnant

Check The Sensor
• The sensor must be fully submerged in water. If the water level is low, fill the aquarium up.
• Move the sensor away from filter outlets and air stones, where bubbles may confuse the reading.
Clean The Sensor
• Algae, slime, and hard water layers can throw readings off.
• Wipe the sensor gently and rinse it. Never scrub the sensor tip.
Calibrate
• Sensor values may drift over time. This is normal and expected.
• Go to Settings → Sensor Calibration and tap the sensor for step-by-step instructions.
Wait After A Water Change
• Readings swing for a while after water changes or new treatments. Give the aquarium an hour to settle before worrying.
SENSOR SPECIFIC

Tips for Each Sensor

Water Level
• The sensor needs flat firm contact with the aquarium wall. Adjust if it has slipped.
• Clear away algae and debris around it.
Temperature
• Ensure the sensor is properly submerged in water.
• Keep it away from the heater to ensure accurate readings.
pH Level
• As this sensor is very sensitive, rinse it with clean water from time to time.
• Never let the sensor tip dry out. If it has dried, it may need replacing.
Total Dissolved Solids
• Ensure the sensor is properly submerged in water.
• Needs steady contact with flowing water. Make sure it is not resting against the glass or gravel.
APP ISSUES

App is Not Updating Data

Check Your Connection
• Confirm your phone has working Wi-Fi or mobile data.
Check The Hub's Connection
• The hub needs your home Wi-Fi. If you changed your network name or password, pair the hub again.
• A hub far from the WiFi router may have a weak signal. Move the router closer or add an extender to fix the connection.
Refresh The App
• Close the app fully and reopen it to refresh the parameter readings.
ALERT ISSUES

Issues with Alerts

Too Many Alerts
• Your safe ranges may be stricter than your aquarium's needs. Widen them in Settings → Water Parameters.
• Turn on Critical alerts only or set Quiet hours in Settings → Notifications.
No Alerts At All
• Check that Maintenance Mode is off in Settings → Notifications. This pauses all alerts for the selected aquarium.
• Ensure that Quiet hours is not covering the time you expect alerts.
• Confirm your phone allows notifications from the AquaSense app.
